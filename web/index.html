Workflow

How physical warehouse sorting works

From arrival dock to inventory forecast in four automated seconds.

1

Scan the tag

An RFID antenna reads the tag attached to the box as it passes the intake conveyor.

2

Confirm arrival

An infrared beam sensor verifies that the box physically arrived and was not just keyed in.

3

Sort by weight

Under 10 kg slides to the left lane; 10 kg or more is directed to the right lane by the servo gate.

4

Know what happens next

The app predicts daily arrival patterns and flags stock that might expire before it is sold.

How sorting works

  1. A tag is scanned at the ESP32 station.
  2. An infrared sensor confirms the parcel physically arrived on the conveyor.
  3. Parcels under 10 kg go left; 10 kg or more goes right via servo diverter gate.
  4. Weights, expiry dates and demand forecasts are simulated.
